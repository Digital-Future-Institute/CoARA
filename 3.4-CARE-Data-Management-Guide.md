## Implementing the CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance


The **CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance** provide guidance for ethical, equitable, and rights-based data practices involving Indigenous Peoples.

CARE complements the **FAIR Principles** by centering **people, purpose, rights, and relationships** in data governance.

Indigenous Data Sovereignty is grounded in the [United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples (UNDRIP)](https://www.un.org/development/desa/indigenouspeoples/wp-content/uploads/sites/19/2018/11/UNDRIP_E_web.pdf), affirming Indigenous PeoplesÔÇÖ rights to control data about their peoples, lands, territories, resources, and knowledge systems.

CARE applies to:

- **Indigenous Knowledge and Traditional Knowledge**  
- **Scientific data involving Indigenous lands, waters, peoples, or resources**  
- **Metadata, genomic data, environmental data, and digital collections**  
- **Historical and contemporary datasets**  

## The CARE Principles

| CARE Principle | Purpose | Key Commitments | Examples / Applications |
|----------------|---------|----------------|--------------------------|
| **C ÔÇô Collective Benefit** | Ensure that data ecosystems generate benefits for Indigenous Peoples and communities. | ÔÇó Support inclusive development and innovation<br>ÔÇó Improve governance and citizen engagement<br>ÔÇó Enable equitable outcomes<br>ÔÇó Align data use with community values<br>ÔÇó Strengthen Indigenous nations and communities | Data initiatives that support community priorities, strengthen local governance, and create social, cultural, environmental, or economic benefits for Indigenous Peoples. |
| **A ÔÇô Authority to Control** | Recognise and empower Indigenous PeoplesÔÇÖ rights and authority over Indigenous data. | ÔÇó Recognise Indigenous rights in data governance<br>ÔÇó Support Indigenous-led decision-making<br>ÔÇó Enable community-defined access protocols<br>ÔÇó Ensure accurate and respectful representation of Indigenous lands, resources, and knowledges<br>ÔÇó Facilitate access to data for governance and self-determination | Indigenous governance frameworks, community-controlled data repositories, culturally appropriate access protocols, and Indigenous-led stewardship models. |
| **R ÔÇô Responsibility** | Ensure that those using Indigenous data act responsibly and support Indigenous self-determination. | ÔÇó Transparent reporting of data use<br>ÔÇó Capacity building and data capability development<br>ÔÇó Strengthen Indigenous languages and cultures<br>ÔÇó Maintain accountability through documentation<br>ÔÇó Engage ethically with communities | Community partnerships, training initiatives, data stewardship plans, public accountability mechanisms, and ongoing consultation processes. |
| **E ÔÇô Ethics** | Prioritise Indigenous rights, wellbeing, and values throughout the data lifecycle. | ÔÇó Minimise harm<br>ÔÇó Maximise benefit<br>ÔÇó Promote justice and equity<br>ÔÇó Respect Indigenous ethical frameworks<br>ÔÇó Support culturally appropriate reuse over time | Ethical review processes, culturally responsive research practices, community consent mechanisms, and long-term stewardship that respects Indigenous values and traditions. |

**CARE Pathway:**

**Collective Benefit ÔåÆ Authority to Control ÔåÆ Responsibility ÔåÆ Ethics**

The CARE Principles complement the FAIR Principles by ensuring that data governance is not only technically effective but also socially just, culturally respectful, and aligned with Indigenous rights and self-determination.


## CARE and FAIR: Complementary Frameworks

FAIR promotes technical data stewardship and open science.  
CARE ensures that openness aligns with Indigenous rights and values.

CARE implementation ensures that:

- Data is **as open as determined by Indigenous communities**
- Data reuse is **purposeful and equitable**
- Open data does not override Indigenous governance
- Cultural metadata and provenance are included
- Historical power imbalances are addressed

CARE is a required dimension of responsible open science.

## Applying CARE Across the Data Lifecycle

CARE principles apply across all stages of research data management.

| Research Stage | CARE-Aligned Actions |
|---------------|---------------------|
| **Planning Stage** | ÔÇó Engage Indigenous communities early<br>ÔÇó Identify whether data involves Indigenous peoples, lands, or knowledge<br>ÔÇó Establish governance agreements<br>ÔÇó Define benefit-sharing mechanisms<br>ÔÇó Document consent and community protocols<br>ÔÇó Plan for cultural metadata and provenance tracking |
| **Data Collection** | ÔÇó Follow community-approved methodologies<br>ÔÇó Respect cultural protocols<br>ÔÇó Ensure informed and ongoing consent<br>ÔÇó Clarify data ownership and authority<br>ÔÇó Avoid extractive practices |
| **Data Processing & Documentation** | ÔÇó Include cultural metadata<br>ÔÇó Record Indigenous provenance<br>ÔÇó Apply community-defined classification systems<br>ÔÇó Use tools such as Traditional Knowledge (TK) Labels where appropriate<br>ÔÇó Document intended use and restrictions |
| **Storage & Access** | ÔÇó Implement access controls aligned with community decisions<br>ÔÇó Distinguish between open, restricted, and community-only data<br>ÔÇó Embed governance rules in metadata<br>ÔÇó Ensure security and sovereignty where possible |
| **Data Sharing & Reuse** | ÔÇó Require ethical review for secondary use<br>ÔÇó Respect community-determined sharing conditions<br>ÔÇó Track reuse and derivative works<br>ÔÇó Ensure attribution and acknowledgement of Indigenous authority<br>ÔÇó Evaluate benefit-sharing outcomes |
| **Long-Term Stewardship** | ÔÇó Maintain community engagement<br>ÔÇó Support ongoing governance oversight<br>ÔÇó Update permissions as contexts change<br>ÔÇó Ensure intergenerational benefit |


### Traditional Knowledge (TK) Labels

TK Labels are digital metadata tags that:

- Restore Indigenous cultural authority
- Improve provenance documentation
- Embed community protocols in metadata
- Support culturally appropriate reuse
- Work within existing catalogues and digital infrastructures

TK Labels demonstrate how CARE and FAIR can operate together.
