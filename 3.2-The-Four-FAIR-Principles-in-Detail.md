## How the FAIR Principles Work Together

The FAIR principles operate synergistically:

- **Findable** data must also be **Accessible** to be useful  
- **Accessible** data gain value when they are **Interoperable**  
- **Interoperable** data become meaningful when they are **Reusable**  
- **Reusable** data depend on all other principles functioning effectively  

FAIR is therefore a holistic framework, not a checklist of isolated requirements.

It guides researchers toward creating datasets that are:

- Robust  
- Discoverable  
- Transparent  
- Adaptable  
- Ethically responsible

**FAIR Pathway:**

**Findable Accessible Interoperable Reusable**

| FAIR Principle | Purpose | Key Mechanisms | Examples / Components |
|----------------|---------|----------------|------------------------|
| **Findable** | Ensure data can be discovered by humans and machines. | Persistent Identifiers (PIDs)<br> Rich, structured metadata<br> Repository storage<br> Human and machine discoverability | **PIDs:** DOIs, Handles<br>**Metadata describe:** content, context, methods, variables, conditions of creation<br>**Repositories enable:** indexing, citation, discovery platform integration |
| **Accessible** | Ensure data and metadata can be reached under clearly defined conditions. | Clear access mechanisms<br> Visible metadata<br> Unambiguous licences and access terms<br> Long-term availability | **Protocols:** HTTP, APIs<br>**Licences:** Creative Commons, restricted-access agreements, clinical governance procedures<br> Metadata remain discoverable even when data are restricted. |
| **Interoperable** | Enable data integration, comparison, and reuse across systems, disciplines, and platforms. |  Standard formats and encodings<br> Controlled vocabularies and ontologies<br> Machine-readable metadata<br> Linked data and identifiers | **Formats:** CSV, XML, JSON, HDF5, TEI-XML, WAV, TIFF<br>**Ontologies:** SNOMED CT, ICD, Gene Ontology, Dublin Core, TEI, DDI<br>**Linked resources:** publications, instruments, samples, datasets |
| **Reusable** | Support responsible, transparent, and long-term reuse of data. | Comprehensive documentation<br> Clear licensing and rights information<br> Defined provenance<br> Ethical and legal compliance<br> Quality assurance | **Documentation:** collection methods, instruments, cleaning procedures, analytical steps<br>**Provenance:** origin, protocols, transformations<br>**Quality:** complete, reliable, transparent about limitations |

Each principle builds on the previous one, creating a pathway from **data discovery** to **long-term scientific and societal reuse**.

## Suggested Next Steps for Researchers and Principle Investigators

- Review your funders' data management requirements  
- Integrate FAIR principles into your Data Management Plan (DMP)  
- Use trusted repositories  
- Assign persistent identifiers  
- Provide structured, rich metadata  
- Clarify licensing and access conditions early  
- Document workflows and provenance throughout the project  

FAIR is not an administrative burden it is a strategic framework for strengthening research quality, integrity, and impact.
