## Introduction

The **[Creative Commons Licences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Commons_license)** and tools were first established in 1991 and are widely used across research, education, culture and heritage sectors. The current licence version [[4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.en)] was updated in 2019 and is internationally recognised.  

**CC licences** offer authors, creators, and organisations a straightforward framework for communicating permissions and requirements for use, establishing clear parameters for reuse and adaptation. Creative Commons licences give everyone, from individual creators to large institutions, a standardised way to grant the public permission to use their creative work under copyright law. 

### Type of material 

**Make sure the material is appropriate for CC licensing**: CC licences are appropriate for all types of content you want to share publicly, [except software and hardware](https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/Frequently_Asked_Questions#Can_I_apply_a_Creative_Commons_license_to_software.3F). (see section of Other open licensing schemes for further information) 

**Specify precisely what it is you are licensing**: Any given work has multiple elements; e.g., text, images, music. Make sure to clearly [mark or indicate in a notice](https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/Marking_your_work_with_a_CC_license) which of those are covered by the licence.

### Before licensing 
Before you apply a CC licence or CC0 to your work, there are some important things to consider: 

- **The licences and CC0 cannot be revoked.** This means once you apply a CC licence to your material, anyone who receives it may rely on that licence for as long as the material is protected by copyright, even if you later stop distributing it. 

- **You must own or control copyright in the work.** Only the copyright holder or someone with express permission from the copyright holder can apply a CC licence or CC0 to a copyrighted work. If you created a work in the scope of your job, you may not be the holder of the copyright. 

You can read about Considerations for licensors and licensees here

## The Creative Commons Licences can be understood as follows: 
---
### CC BY ÔÇö Attribution
![CC BY](https://mirrors.creativecommons.org/presskit/buttons/88x31/svg/by.svg)  
**CCBY Attribution CCBY licence:** Requires attribution, allowing others to use, modify, and distribute the work (even commercially) as long as they give credit to the original creator. This is often considered the most open and permissive of the Creative Commons licences.  

---

### CC BY-SA ÔÇö Attribution ShareAlike
![CC BY-SA](https://mirrors.creativecommons.org/presskit/buttons/88x31/svg/by-sa.svg)  
**CCBYSA share alike CCBY-SA licence:** Similar to CC BY, but also requires derivative works to be shared under the same licence (ShareAlike). This ensures that any modified versions of the work retain the same freedoms as the original.  


---

### Other Creative Commons licences provide up front permissions on use while retaining most of the rights recognised by copyright law. 

---

### CC BY-NC ÔÇö Attribution NonCommercial
![CC BY-NC](https://mirrors.creativecommons.org/presskit/buttons/88x31/svg/by-nc.svg)  
**Attribution Non Commercia CCBY-NC licence:** Prohibits commercial reuse. Users can use, modify, and distribute the work non-commercially as long as they give credit to the original creator. This is commonly used when creators want to share their work but restrict commercial exploitation.

---

### CC BY-ND ÔÇö Attribution NoDerivatives
![CC BY-ND](https://mirrors.creativecommons.org/presskit/buttons/88x31/svg/by-nd.svg)  
**Attribution No Derivatives CCBY-ND licence:** Prohibits modification. Allows others to use and distribute the work (even commercially) as long as they give credit to the original creator and do not make any changes or derivative works (e.g., translating, remixing, adapting, or building upon the original).

---

### CC BY-NC-SA ÔÇö Attribution NonCommercial ShareAlike
![CC BY-NC-SA](https://mirrors.creativecommons.org/presskit/buttons/88x31/svg/by-nc-sa.svg)  
**Attribution Non Commercial Share Alike CCBY-NC-SA licence:** Prohibits other activities in combination, merging elements of CC BY-NC and CC BY-SA, allowing others to use, modify, and distribute the work non-commercially as long as they give credit to the original creator and share their derivative works under the same non-commercial terms.

---

### CC BY-NC-ND ÔÇö Attribution NonCommercial NoDerivatives
![CC BY-NC-ND](https://mirrors.creativecommons.org/presskit/buttons/88x31/svg/by-nc-nd.svg)  
**Attribution Non Commercial, No derivates CCBY-NC-ND licence:** The most restrictive licence, which permits others to use and share the work non-commercially with attribution, but prohibits any alterations or adaptations to the original work.
